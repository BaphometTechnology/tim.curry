Where It Started

Stage & Theatre

Long before Hollywood, Curry was a stage actor — and he never fully left. His theatre career spans the Royal Shakespeare Company, the original Rocky Horror Show, and two Tony-nominated Broadway performances.

Early 1970s

Royal Shakespeare Company & Repertory Theatre

Company member

Curry trained in British repertory theatre before his breakout, developing the stage presence that would define his career.

1973

The Rocky Horror Show

Dr. Frank-N-Furter · Royal Court Theatre, London

Curry originated the role of the sweet transvestite scientist in Richard O'Brien's cult musical, an instant sensation that launched him to stardom.

1974–75

The Rocky Horror Show

Dr. Frank-N-Furter · King's Road Theatre & Roxy Theatre, Los Angeles

The production transferred and toured, with Curry reprising the role before taking it to film in 1975.

1980–81

Amadeus

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart · Broadway, Broadhurst Theatre

Curry originated the role of Mozart opposite Ian McKellen's Salieri in Peter Shaffer's play, earning a Tony Award nomination for Best Featured Actor in a Play.

1992

My Favorite Year

Alan Swann · Broadway, Lincoln Center

Curry led this musical adaptation of the 1982 film as a swashbuckling, hard-drinking movie star navigating the golden age of live television.

2005

Monty Python's Spamalot

King Arthur · Broadway, Shubert Theatre

Curry originated King Arthur in Eric Idle's Tony-winning Best Musical, receiving his second Tony Award nomination for Best Actor in a Musical.
