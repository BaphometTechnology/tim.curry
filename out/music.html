A Voice for Song

Music

Long before “Sweet Transvestite” became a showstopper, Curry was a trained singer — and he kept recording throughout his acting career.

Solo Albums

1978

Read My Lips

Curry's debut solo album, leaning into glam and rock and roll and produced with the theatrical flair of his stage work.

1979

Fearless

His second studio album, featuring the single "I Do the Rock," which became a modest chart hit and remains a cult favorite.

Soundtracks & Recordings

The Rocky Horror Picture Show Soundtrack

Curry's performances of "Sweet Transvestite" and "I Can Make You a Man," among others, remain the definitive versions of Richard O'Brien's songs.

Spoken-Word & Narration

Beyond music, Curry lent his distinctive voice to numerous audiobook and narration projects, including recordings of classic children's stories, earning him a Grammy Award nomination for his work.
